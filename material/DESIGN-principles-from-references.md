# Design principles read on the reference pages

| Rank | Source | Section | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hermès | Every block of the page | One single gap separates every block from the next, 48px below 1024px and 64px above, applied uniformly instead of a value chosen per section. |
| 2 | Hermès | Hermès, acteur économique et social | A text block is centred and pushed far away from the edges, 15px of margin plus 20px of padding on mobile, then a 12% inset above 1024px, then a fixed 150px inset above 1290px, inside a container capped at 1258px. |
| 3 | Hermès | Créativité, innovation et singularité au cœur de la stratégie | The section title and the paragraph under it use the same serif family in the same italic, and only a size step from 16-20px to 30-34px plus a 40 point weight step separate them. |
| 4 | Hermès | Une maison d'artisans aux valeurs humanistes | A text block holds exactly one title, one short paragraph and one link, and never a second idea. |
| 5 | Hermès | Hermès dans le monde | Section titles are set in sentence case with no letter spacing, against the uppercase letter spaced default the rest of the site uses for the same heading level. |
| 6 | Hermès | Six générations d'artisans | Inside a text block every element but the first carries 16px below it and the last carries none, so a block of several lines has one internal rhythm and no exception. |
| 7 | Hermès | Hermès, acteur économique et social | The call to action is a text link underlined by a 1px rule, set at the smallest size of the page and placed 16px below the paragraph on mobile and 24px above 1024px, never a filled button. |
| 8 | Chanel | COLLECTION N°5, LES SIGNATURES DE CHANEL | The page title runs from 25px on mobile to 40px above 961px, uppercase, with letter spacing growing from 0.09rem to 0.15rem as the size grows. |
| 9 | Chanel | COLLECTION N°5, LES SIGNATURES DE CHANEL | The paragraph under the title is set at caption size, 11px to 12px, about a third of the title, so the hierarchy is carried by the gap between the two sizes rather than by a large body. |
| 10 | Chanel | COLLECTION N°5, LES SIGNATURES DE CHANEL | Side gutters are a percentage of the viewport, 5.33% on mobile and about 8% above, inside a container capped at 1441px, so the margin grows with the screen instead of staying fixed. |
| 11 | Chanel | COLLECTION N°5, LES SIGNATURES DE CHANEL | The title block is capped at 792px and aligned to the top left of its section rather than centred in it. |
| 12 | Hermès | Six générations d'artisans | A standard image sits in the very same inset column as the text, and only the opening hero is allowed to widen to the full 1258px container. |
| 13 | Hermès | Hermès, acteur économique et social | The block that follows a text block loses its top gap entirely, so the image reads as belonging to the text above it rather than as a new section. |
| 14 | Hermès | Une maison d'artisans aux valeurs humanistes | Two images shown together take 50% of the width each with a 20px inner gutter above 1024px, and stack in the same order with a 30px gap below, with no third arrangement. |
| 15 | Hermès | A propos d'Hermès | The opening block stacks a short eyebrow line above the title, both inside the same text block laid over the image. |
| 16 | Chanel | BROCHE N°5 SIGNATURE BOTTLE | Every image carries its own caption in 11px uppercase letter spaced text, placed at the bottom centre over the visual itself. |
| 17 | Chanel | BAGUE N°5 DROP, BOUCLES D'OREILLES N°5 DROP | Images run edge to edge with no gutter at all while text blocks keep theirs, so the two kinds of block are told apart by their width alone. |
| 18 | Chanel | BAGUE N°5 DROP, BAGUE ETERNAL N°5, COLLIER RAS-DE-COU ETERNAL N°5 | Two full width images placed one under the other are butted with a -1px top margin so no hairline of background ever shows between them. |
| 19 | Chanel | Découvrir la collection N°5 | The single closing link is given deliberately unequal air, 50px above and 73px below on mobile and 70px above and 103px below on desktop, so more space falls after it than before. |
| 20 | Chanel | Découvrez les 6 collections | A set of equal items is laid out as a grid of 2 columns below 601px and 3 above, with a gap of only 5px so the images almost touch. |
| 21 | Hermès | Six générations d'artisans | A change of background to white marks a change of chapter, with the same 48px and 64px rhythm kept across the join. |
| 22 | Hermès | A propos d'Hermès | Mobile and desktop are served two different crops of the same photograph rather than one image scaled to both. |
| 23 | Chanel | COLLECTION N°5, LES SIGNATURES DE CHANEL | The title is one element holding two lines, the first set one step smaller than the second, so the smaller line reads as a label of the larger. |
| 24 | Chanel | COLLECTION N°5, LES SIGNATURES DE CHANEL | The opening title section is sized in viewport height, 52vh above 601px and 82vh above 961px, instead of being sized by the text it holds. |
